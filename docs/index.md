# Canadian SAR Technology Hub

A community-maintained directory of field-tested technologies, operational templates, and technical best practices for Canadian Ground Search and Rescue (GSAR).

All content is compiled from the real-world experiences of active searchers, incident commanders, and technical specialists across Canada.

## Community & Editorial Standards

- **Field-Tested by Practitioners**: Every tool, guide, and workflow featured here reflects real field experience in Canadian operational environments.
- **Independent & Non-Commercial**: This directory does not accept sponsored content, vendor promotions, or marketing pitches.
- **Balanced Guidance**: Commercial tools widely adopted by SAR teams are featured with honest operational feedback, noting limitations, field gotchas, and budget realities.
