# Training & Standards

This section references official standards, training curricula, drill scenarios, and competency frameworks across Canada.

---

## Subcategories & Focus Areas
- **National Standards**: CSA Z1620 (Core competency curriculum for GSAR) and related CSA SAR standards
- **Provincial Association Frameworks**: EMCR/BCSARA (BC), SARVAC, OSARVA (Ontario), SARNCO (Nova Scotia), etc.
- **Team Drills & Field Exercises**: Mock incident scenarios, table-top exercises, night navigation challenges
- **Evaluations**: Practical evaluation checklists, tracking sign evaluation rubrics
