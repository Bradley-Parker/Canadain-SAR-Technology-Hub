# Medical & Evacuation

This section covers medical guidance, patient packaging techniques, and evacuation equipment standards.

---

## Subcategories & Focus Areas
- **Cold Weather & Hypothermia**: Cold water immersion protocols, thermal wraps (burrito / hypowrap standards)
- **Patient Packaging**: Litter / basket rigging, spinal immobilization in wilderness terrain, vacuum mattresses
- **Medical Documentation**: Wilderness triage sheets, patient handover protocols for BCAS / paramedic services
- **Evacuation Rigging**: Steep-angle rope lower/haul systems, wheel attachments for litters
