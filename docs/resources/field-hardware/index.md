# Field Hardware & Equipment

This section covers technological hardware deployed by ground searchers, dog handlers, and specialized technical teams.

---

## Subcategories & Focus Areas
- **RPAS & Drones**: Search drone models, flight planning software, visual/thermal payload standards
- **Night Vision & Thermal**: Handheld FLIR, monoculars, detection envelopes in heavy canopy
- **Tracking & K9 Telemetry**: GPS collars, live tracking relays, sign-cutting equipment
- **Power & Field Charging**: Solar generators, battery management in sub-zero environments
