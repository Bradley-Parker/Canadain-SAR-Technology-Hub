# Team Administration

Running a SAR team takes more than boots on the ground. Between D4H records, tax credit letters, ID cards, attendance sheets, and mileage tracking, the administrative load on team managers can be significant — especially for volunteer organizations where that work falls on a small number of dedicated people.

This section covers tools and workflows that help reduce that burden, so admins spend less time at a desk and more time focused on readiness.

---

## Focus Areas

- **D4H Integration Tools**: Software that extends or automates what D4H already tracks for your team.
- **Member Records & Compliance**: Tax credit documentation, ID cards, and attendance records.
- **Reimbursement & Mileage Tracking**: Tools that help calculate and document member expenses.
