# Community Contribution Guide

The **Canadian SAR Technology Hub** is built by and for Canadian Ground Search and Rescue volunteers and professionals. 

If your team has developed an effective template, discovered a great workflow, or evaluated equipment in the field, we encourage you to share your knowledge with other teams.

---

## Our Values & Editorial Standards

Before contributing, please review our **[Editorial Standards & Core Values](editorial-standards.md)**:
- **Field-Tested**: Content must reflect genuine operational or field-training experience.
- **Vendor-Neutral**: We do not publish sponsored content or unproven vendor pitches.
- **Pragmatic Reviews**: Commercial tools used by SAR teams are welcome when accompanied by candid, objective field feedback (including limitations and costs).

---

## 3 Easy Ways to Share or Update Content

### 1. The Simplest Way: "Edit This Page" Button
If you notice outdated information, a broken link, or have an operational tip to add to an existing page:
1. Navigate to that page on this website.
2. Click the **pencil icon** (:material-pencil:) at the top right of the page.
3. Edit the text in your browser and click **Commit changes** (GitHub will automatically guide you through a Pull Request).

---

### 2. Submit a New Resource via GitHub Web
If you want to add a new tool, procedure, or document:
1. Navigate to the [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Bradley-Parker/Canadain-SAR-Technology-Hub).
2. Browse to `docs/resources/<category>/`.
3. Click **Add file** -> **Create new file**.
4. Name your file with a descriptive, lowercase name ending in `.md` (for example: `caltopo-incident-template.md`).
5. Copy the contents of the **[Resource Template](template.md)** into the editor.
6. Fill in your details and click **Propose changes**.

---

### 3. Non-Technical Web Form (No Git Required)
If you prefer not to touch Markdown or Git directly:
1. Open a **[New Resource Submission Form](https://github.com/Bradley-Parker/Canadain-SAR-Technology-Hub/issues/new?template=new-resource.yml)**.
2. Fill out the plain-English form fields (Title, Category, Province, Description, and drag-and-drop any attachments).
3. Click **Submit new issue**. Community maintainers will review and format it for you.
