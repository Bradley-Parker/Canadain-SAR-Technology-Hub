# Editorial Standards & Core Values

The Canadian SAR Technology Hub exists to provide reliable, objective, and field-tested information to ground searchers and rescue teams. To maintain the trust of the Canadian SAR community, all contributions must uphold these standards.

---

## 1. Grounded in Operational Experience

Every tool, software template, or procedure shared in this hub should reflect **real-world experience from search and rescue personnel**. 

- Resources must be tested in actual search operations or rigorous field training exercises.
- We focus on practical efficacy: How does this tool perform in cold weather? In thick canopy? With limited battery? Under high operational stress?
- Unverified tools, conceptual prototypes, or speculative workflows without field testing are not published here.

---

## 2. Independent & Non-Commercial

This hub is built by searchers, for searchers. 

- **No Sponsored Posts / No Sales Pipeline**: This hub is strictly non-commercial. There are no sponsored posts, paid endorsements, or marketing copy.
- **Affiliate Links Policy**:
  Affiliate links are permitted only when clearly disclosed. Any proceeds must directly benefit the contributor's SAR team, SAR organization, or another community-serving initiative. Affiliate links must never intentionally provide a worse price or experience than a standard purchase link. Resource listings and recommendations must remain unbiased regardless of affiliate relationships.

  *Example format:*
  > **Official Website**: `https://example.com/product`  
  > **Affiliate Link** *(Benefits York Sunbury Ground Search & Rescue)*: `https://affiliate.example.com/product`

- **No Direct Vendor Marketing**: We do not publish product descriptions or promotional material written directly by commercial vendors.
- **Volunteer Run**: The hub is an independent, community-driven repository with no commercial revenue model.

---

## 3. How We Cover Paid & Commercial Tools

Ground SAR teams across Canada frequently depend on commercial software and hardware—including satellite communication systems, specialized mapping platforms, thermal imagers, and drone hardware.

Commercial and proprietary tools are welcome here, under clear conditions:

| We Encourage | We Avoid |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Practitioner Reviews**: Honest feedback from teams actively deploying the tool on operations. | **Marketing Copy**: Vendor spec sheets that repeat promotional claims without field validation. |
| **Balanced Assessments**: Clear notes on limitations, subscription costs, durability issues, and learning curves. | **Uncritical Endorsements**: Presenting any single commercial product as the "only" solution. |
| **Open Formats & Data Ownership**: Favoring tools that allow teams to export and own their operational data (GPX, KML, CSV). | **Walled Gardens**: Workflows that lock teams into proprietary, non-portable ecosystems without open export options. |

---

## 4. The Canadian SAR Operational Context

Technology must be evaluated with Canadian search conditions in mind:
- **Harsh Environments**: Sub-zero winter operations, extreme wet coastal weather, and remote wilderness where power and communications are absent.
- **Interoperability**: Compatibility with Canadian mutual aid partners, provincial tasking agencies (e.g. RCMP, OPP, Sûreté du Québec, Parks Canada), and national guidelines (CSA Z1620).
- **Cost Realities**: Most Canadian GSAR teams are volunteer-based non-profits operating on limited grant funding and community donations. Affordability, licensing models, and long-term maintenance costs are critical considerations.
